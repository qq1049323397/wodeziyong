# Earth 2036 archive frames

Six generated WebP frames used by the `灾前地球文件` interactive archive UI.

- `frame-01.webp`: isolated high-tech society
- `frame-02.webp`: humanoid research laboratory
- `frame-03.webp`: human-humanoid coexistence
- `frame-04.webp`: anti-humanoid conflict
- `frame-05.webp`: magnolia disease clinical record
- `frame-06.webp`: ruined city and oasis era

The character card loads these assets through commit-pinned jsDelivr URLs.
